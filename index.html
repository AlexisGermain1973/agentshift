Agentshift

Support

Agentshift simulates the reorganisation of a company around AI agents: who they replace, what it saves, what it costs, and in which order. It runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad, without an account or a server.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Get started.
  2. Tap New company and follow the four steps: identity, headcount and payroll, organisation, ambition. You can also tap Load the examples to explore two sample companies.
  3. Read the plan: indicators, charts, Who replaces whom, roadmap, deployed agents. Adjust assumptions at any time; the plan is recomputed instantly.
  4. Share the PDF report from the ⋯ menu.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my companies stored? On your device only, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Are the numbers real? They are orders of magnitude from an editable model, meant to start a discussion. Agentshift is neither legal advice nor a decision.

Why don't I see the Apple Intelligence button? It only appears on devices where Apple's on-device language model is available. Otherwise the deterministic memo is shown.

Which languages? English and French, following your device settings.

Pour commencer

  1. Ouvrez l'app et touchez Commencer.
  2. Touchez Nouvelle entreprise et suivez les quatre étapes : identité, effectif et masse salariale, organisation, ambition. Charger les exemples ouvre deux entreprises de démonstration.

  4. Partagez le rapport PDF depuis le menu ⋯.
